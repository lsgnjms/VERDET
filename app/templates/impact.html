How the estimates are calculated

diesel_L = kWh × {{ diesel_factor }} L/kWh
CO₂_kg  = diesel_L × {{ co2_factor }} kg/L

{{ diesel_factor }} L/kWh is a typical fuel use for a small diesel generator; {{ co2_factor }} kg is the CO₂ released by burning one litre of diesel. Both factors are configurable and conservative. These are estimates, not certified offsets.
